# Sibyl brand mark

## Primary illustrated logo

`sibyl-figure-logo-v1.png` is the primary illustrated Sibyl mark. It is derived from the supplied historical engraving and preserves the figure's raised hand, face direction, drapery, standing pose, and stone base.

The cleaned version uses near-black ink (`#17201B`) on warm paper (`#F4F0E6`) to match the existing interface. It removes the original plate border, title, captions, stains, wrinkles, and photographic texture. The robe was extended to make the figure fully clothed and suitable for general public use.

Use this full illustrated mark at 96 px or larger. It is too detailed for favicons or very small header icons; derive and approve a separate simplified small mark before replacing the current compact `S` symbol.

Do not add gradients, shadows, decorative frames, or unrelated symbols. Do not recolor it independently from the interface palette.
