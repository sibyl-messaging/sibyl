Punch Card Demo

Phone-browser demo of the Ghost Sheet concept. No login, no backend.

How This Works (Plain English)

This demo splits reading and typing into two visual layers: what your screen shows (mostly random noise), and what your paper sheet reveals (the signal). Your paper acts like a physical key.

What You Do

  1. Write a message at the top (example: HELLO.).
  2. Open 1) Make Mask, place blank paper on your phone, and build a negative mask: labeled cells = leave blank white, unlabeled cells = fill dark black.
  3. Open 2) Read, place that mask back on screen, then align it with the arrow controls: Center first, then tap arrows one step at a time until letters become clear through white windows. Use Strobe only to help your eyes catch alignment.
  4. Open 3) Type, find a clear letter window on your paper, read the 2-digit number under that window, and type that number on the keypad.

What The App Is Doing Behind The Scenes

  • It turns your message into a list of grid positions (hole IDs), not readable text.
  • In the read view, it paints a black/white noise grid, but keeps specific positions bright to carry signal.
  • In the type view, it assigns random 00-99 codes to each active hole.
  • After every 2-digit entry, it reshuffles the mapping so old numbers become useless.

This page is a concept demo for UX/physics testing. It is not the full secure mobile app flow.

Read Through Overlay

Exact flow: remove paper after Step 1, open this tab, then place paper back with the traced TOP EDGE against the top of the phone.

  1. Tap Center first.
  2. Tap arrow buttons slowly until letters sharpen in the white windows.
  3. If it still looks noisy, toggle Strobe, then continue nudging with arrows.
  4. Success signal: you can read stable letters. Failure signal: only random speckle.

Trace Test

This mode is a best-effort phone tracing surface: static image, fullscreen request, orientation lock request, wake lock request, no page scrolling, and a touch shield over the page.

  1. Use any paper. Fold one edge over the top of your phone like a hinge, then trace the on-screen TOP EDGE guide.
  2. Choose Public, Share A, or Share B.
  3. Tap Start Trace Mode, then stop touching the phone.
  4. Trace dots or short slits only. Do not try to freehand long lines.
